## Final design prompt summary

Edit the reference logo and simplify it drastically to the essential maple-leaf silhouette and a short curved stem. Use a flat, natural autumn gradient that flows from green through yellow and orange to red. Keep only one curved central vein and two short side strokes. Add one thin wind arc to suggest gentle motion. Remove texture, facets, three-dimensional effects, gloss, highlights, and extra detail. Keep the background transparent, with a clear silhouette readable at small app-icon sizes.
